MineNight Media Role
====================

Built-in group key: media
Display name in administration menus: Media
Nametag prefix: <#E52DBB>📹</#E52DBB><#00C8F0>+</#00C8F0>
Camera: exact video-camera emoji U+1F4F9, pink #E52DBB
Plus: literal default-font + sign, cyan #00C8F0 (built as its own Adventure text component, not MiniMessage markup)
Spacing: no gap between camera and plus; no gap between plus and player name
Player name color: #E52DBB
Priority: 25

The in-world nametag intentionally does NOT show the word MEDIA. It shows
only the pink video-camera symbol, the cyan plus sign, and the pink player name.
The video-camera symbol and plus are formatted as one compact prefix so both
use the same text baseline. The old private-use camera glyph is migrated away.

On startup, MineNightPerms migrates an older Media prefix to the exact requested
video-camera emoji and clears a separate legacy emoji field to prevent duplicate
or extra spacing. Default white role/name colors are migrated to pink. Other
custom administrator color choices are preserved. The group display name stays
Media in the management GUI.

Assign a player from console or as a MineNightPerms administrator:
/mnperms player set role <player> media

The role can also be assigned through /minenightperms -> Assign Roles.
The role is not automatically assigned to any player.

Nametag rendering implementation (v1.8.6):
- The camera and plus are built as separate Adventure components.
- The plus is the exact plain ASCII character "+" using the default Minecraft font, colored cyan.
- No bold, italic, scale, custom glyph, or MiniMessage decoration is applied to the plus.
