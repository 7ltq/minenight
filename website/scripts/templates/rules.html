These rules apply to MineNight gameplay, community interactions and Support. Game modes may display additional instructions; follow those instructions and reasonable staff directions.

1. Respect other players

No harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexual exploitation or targeted abuse. Do not share another person's private information. Keep usernames, nicknames, skins, builds and messages appropriate for the community.

2. Play fairly

No hacked clients, combat automation, unfair macros, unauthorized movement/combat assistance, duplication or other unfair advantages. Accessibility tools that do not create an unfair advantage are not automatically prohibited; ask staff if uncertain about a specific tool.

3. Do not exploit bugs

Do not abuse glitches, bypass restrictions, duplicate items or disrupt events. Report vulnerabilities privately through Support or contact@minenight.de. Do not publish instructions that put the service or other users at risk.

4. Protect accounts and the service

No account theft, phishing, malware, denial-of-service attacks, unauthorized access or attempts to steal credentials. Do not ask players for passwords or tokens, impersonate staff or evade bans and mutes through alternate accounts.

5. Keep chat useful

No spam, flooding, scams, malicious links, unsolicited advertising or repeated disruptive messages. Use the appropriate channels. Do not encourage harassment or misuse mentions.

6. Follow match and event rules

Follow the rules displayed for your arena, Battle Royale or event. No match fixing, unauthorized teaming, intentional interference or evasion of event limits. A rank does not exempt anyone from fair-play rules or moderation.

7. Submit honest reports

Provide relevant context and evidence. Do not fabricate screenshots, make knowingly false reports, spam tickets or upload material unrelated to a case. Redact unnecessary personal information. Staff approval is required for rank benefits that explicitly say so, including custom announcements and promotional pings.

8. Moderation and appeals

Violations can lead to proportionate warnings, mutes, kicks, suspensions or bans. Staff assess evidence, severity and repeated conduct; urgent protective action may precede a full review. If you believe a decision is wrong, contact Support or contact@minenight.de with your Minecraft username and relevant details. Discuss the case privately instead of targeting individual staff members.

See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for service and data-handling information.
