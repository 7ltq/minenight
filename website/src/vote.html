    GOOD TO KNOW

    A little help
    before you vote.

    Voting is handled by each listing website. Use your exact Minecraft username and follow its instructions.

    How do I vote?

    Open a voting site above, enter your Minecraft Java username and complete that site's voting steps. Then come back here to visit the other lists.

    How often can I vote?

    Each server list sets its own voting interval. Check the message on that website to find out when you can vote again.

    Do I need a MineNight account?

    No website account is needed. Minecraft account features on MineNight are Coming Soon. A voting service may have its own sign-in requirements.

    A voting site won't open. What can I do?

    Some lists use a security check or may be temporarily unavailable. Try again later or contact the MineNight team on Discord. You can still use the other voting links.
