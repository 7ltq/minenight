6. Support tickets, contact and attachments

We store Discord ID, display name, entered Minecraft username, verified role and team-access status; ticket category, title, messages, status, close/reopen reasons and timestamps; and any images, videos or TikTok application links you submit. Message authors retain a snapshot of their verified name and role at sending time. Login/abuse controls can temporarily record request IP addresses. Email enquiries include the sender address, message and technical mail data.

Tickets and uploads are stored outside the public document root. Ticket access is limited to the creator and authorized team members. New-ticket notifications send the title, category, username and private ticket link to the Discord team channel; the notification does not attach the full conversation or files. Authorized staff review reports, investigate issues and respond. Closing or reopening a ticket queues a Discord direct message to its creator containing the ticket reference, action, reason and acting team member. Discord privacy settings may prevent delivery. Permanently deleting a ticket does not send a direct message. Avoid sending passwords, tokens, payment-card data, unrelated personal information or sensitive material.

Handling requested assistance is based on Article 6(1)(b) where applicable; abuse prevention and handling reports rely on Article 6(1)(f). [Confirm email-host processing arrangements and any different basis for a particular case.]

Staff applications additionally contain your age in years, previous experience, weekly availability, time zone, and answers about microphone access, screen sharing and English communication. We use these answers to review your application and discuss it with you. The application remains a private ticket; its notification and link are sent to the Discord staff-applications channel. The closed-ticket retention period below also applies to staff applications. Do not provide your full date of birth, address or school.

For application approval, we link your Discord account to a Minecraft UUID and username using a single-use code entered in-game. Codes expire after 10 minutes. We record the approving Owner, selected rank and delivery status to grant the chosen Discord and Minecraft roles and investigate delivery errors. Account links remain with your Support profile until deletion; approval records follow the associated ticket retention. Access is restricted to the applicant and authorized staff. The intended bases are Article 6(1)(b) for the requested account service and Article 6(1)(f) for preventing impersonation and maintaining an approval audit trail.
