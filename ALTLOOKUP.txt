MineNight All-in-One 1.6.3 - IP-based Alt Lookup

/altlookup <player> - staff only

Configuration: plugins/MineNightAllInOne/moderation/config.yml
  ip-detection:
    enabled: true
    auto-ban-matching-ips: true
    exempt-uuids: []

If an incoming player matches the last recorded IP hash of a player with an active ban,
the incoming account inherits the same ban deadline (including permanent bans).
The inherited ban is cleared when its source ban is manually removed or expires.

NOTE: Shared IP does not prove account ownership. Siblings, schools, VPNs and
shared networks may share IP addresses. Review /altlookup results before
assuming two names belong to one player.

Build by running build.bat in the MineNightAllInOne folder.
