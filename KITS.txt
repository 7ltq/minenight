MineNight Kits v1.6.8
/kit - nine saved loadouts and personal enderchests.
Left-click kit chest: equip saved kit.
Right-click kit chest: inspect its loadout; Save Inventory to Kit saves current hotbar, inventory, armor and offhand.
Eye of Ender below each kit: personal 27-slot enderchest for that kit; stored in kits/players.yml.
/kitroom - shared 3-page item selection for every player (45 items/page). Items can be taken without draining the template.
/staffkitroom - staff-only editor for the 3 shared pages. Changes saved when changing pages or closing.
/k1 to /k9, /kit1 to /kit9 - equip kit.
/copy kit <player> <number> - copy a saved kit.
/regear - obtain single-use regear shulker.


Shared /kitroom inventory interaction (v1.8.8):
- Players can use normal clicks and drags to rearrange items in their own inventory while /kitroom is open.
- Clicking one of the top 45 shared item slots still takes a copy without removing the shared template item.
- Shift-click and collect-to-cursor are blocked to protect the shared template from being changed.
