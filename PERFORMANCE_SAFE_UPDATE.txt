- Item despawn: spawn location resolved once per sweep rather than for each item; the 2s/10s rules and scheduling are unchanged.

- Crystal/anchor combat and explosions: NO changes, to avoid interference with FasterCrystals.
- Death kit, compact loot, TPA, moderation and GUI: NO changes.
