MineNight Vanish

Command: /vanish
Permission: minenight.staff.vanish (default: op)

When enabled:
- The player can fly until vanish is disabled.
- The player's previous flight/allow-flight state is restored when vanish is disabled.
- Every non-operator is unable to see the vanished player.
- Server operators can still see the vanished player.
- New non-operator joins are also kept from seeing players who are vanished.
- The attached custom nametag display is hidden/suspended to avoid a floating label revealing the player.
- Quit announcements are suppressed if the player disconnects while vanished.

Vanish state is session-only. Disconnecting or disabling the plugin clears it.
